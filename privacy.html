Privacy policy

Hermes agent · last updated 10 October 2026

Hermes agent is a private assistant used by one person, its owner. The only Google accounts it connects to are accounts that belong to the owner. This page explains what it does with data from those accounts.

What data is accessed

After the owner signs in and gives permission, the assistant can access, in the connected Google account:

  • Gmail — messages and their attachments, labels;
  • Google Calendar — calendars and events;
  • Google Drive — files and folders, including Google Docs and Sheets;
  • Contacts — names and addresses, read only.

The owner can grant fewer permissions on Google's consent screen; the assistant then works only with what was granted.

How the data is used

The data is used only to carry out the owner's own requests: to read mail the owner forwards and turn it into notes and tasks, to read and create calendar events, and to read or save files the owner asks for. It is not used for advertising, profiling or any purpose unrelated to these requests.

Where the data is stored

The assistant runs on a computer owned by the owner. The sign-in token that Google issues is stored on that computer, in a file readable only by the owner's user account. Notes the assistant makes from mail, calendar or files are kept in the owner's private notes on the same computer.

Who the data is shared with

The data is not sold and is not given to anyone for their own use. To answer a request, the text needed for that request may be sent to the AI model providers the owner has chosen to run the assistant; they process it to produce the answer. Nothing else leaves the owner's computer.

Keeping and deleting data

Data stays in the owner's Google account unless the owner asks the assistant to change it. The owner can remove the assistant's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the stored sign-in token then stops working and can be deleted from the computer. Notes made earlier remain the owner's own files and can be deleted by the owner.

Other people

Nobody other than the owner can sign in to Hermes agent. Mail the owner receives from other people is handled as part of the owner's mailbox, under the rules above.
